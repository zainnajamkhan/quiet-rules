Quiet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Quiet does not collect anything about you.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no crash reporting service. Nothing you do in Quiet is sent anywhere, and there is no server that could receive it.

What Quiet stores, and where

Everything Quiet knows about your settings stays on your Mac, in Quiet's own container:

  • Which parts of which websites you have chosen to hide
  • Which websites you have blocked
  • Which applications you have blocked

Deleting the app removes all of it. None of it is ever transmitted.

The one network request

Quiet makes exactly one kind of network request: it periodically downloads an updated list of rules describing which parts of supported websites to hide. Websites change their layout, and this is how Quiet keeps working without waiting for an app update.

That request:

  • Is an ordinary download of a public file, the same file for every user
  • Sends no information about you, your settings, or the sites you visit
  • Happens at most once a day
  • Can fail with no consequence beyond Quiet continuing to use the rules it already has

What the Safari extension can see

The extension needs permission to run on the pages you visit, because hiding part of a page requires being on that page. It reads the structure of a page only to apply the rules you have switched on, entirely on your Mac, as the page loads.

It does not record, store, or transmit your browsing history, page contents, form contents, keystrokes, or anything else. There is nowhere for that information to go.

Accessibility permission

If you use app blocking, macOS asks you to grant Accessibility permission. Quiet uses it for one purpose: to hide the windows of applications you have chosen to block. It is not used to read screen contents or to observe anything you type.

Purchases

Quiet Pro is a one time purchase handled entirely by Apple. Quiet receives only whether a purchase exists. It never sees your name, email address, or payment details.

Children

Quiet is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
