Quiet

Keep the sites you need. Lose the parts that eat you.

Quiet is a Mac app and Safari extension. It hides the parts of websites built to keep you scrolling, and blocks the sites and apps you would rather not open at all.

What it does

  • Hides feeds, recommendations, comments and other engagement machinery on supported sites
  • Blocks websites outright, with a replacement page rather than a browser error
  • Hides distracting Mac apps while blocking is on
  • No accounts, no analytics, no subscription

Rules

Websites change their layout, so the rules that decide what to hide live in a file Quiet downloads rather than inside the app. That file is published here and is the same for everyone.
